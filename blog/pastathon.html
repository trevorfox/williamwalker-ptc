The Pastathon is on: dine at Pastini on Tuesday, September 15

September 13, 2026 · William Walker PTC

Pastini Cedar Hills Crossing is turning Tuesday into a fundraiser for William Walker. Dine in or take out on Tuesday, September 15, mention William Walker Elementary when you order, and Pastini will donate 20% of your table's sales to the PTC. That is it. No flyer, no code, no sign-up.

How it works

  • 🗓️ When: Tuesday, September 15, all day. Pastini is open from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • 📍 Where: Pastini Cedar Hills Crossing, 3487 SW Cedar Hills Blvd, Beaverton. Get directions.
  • 🗣️ What to do: Tell your server or the person at the counter that you are with William Walker. That is what makes your table a participating table.
  • 🥡 Takeout counts too. Order online or call (503) 619-2241, then mention William Walker when you pick up.

Planning ahead? Browse the menu or check hours and location details.

Make it a big night

Every participating table adds up, so the more of us who show up, the more the school earns.

  • Bring the extended family. Grandparents, neighbors, and coworkers all count. Anyone who mentions William Walker is in.
  • Go early or go takeout. Pastini does not take reservations, so if the wait is long, order to go and eat at home.
  • Spread the word. Forward this post or just text a few families from your kid's class.

Thank you to Pastini for hosting us. Please tell them so when you are there.

Walkerthon is less than two weeks away

Walkerthon, our biggest fundraiser of the year, is Thursday, September 24. Fundraising is happening right now, and it is the pledges that come in before the run that get us to our goal of $40,000.

  • 📩 Packets are home. Ask family, friends, and favorite local businesses. Have your kid make a short video. Check whether your employer matches donations.
  • 💳 Donate online through Zeffy, or by cash or check. All donations are tax deductible, and Zeffy sends a receipt automatically. Our tax ID is 26-3542951.
  • 🎟️ Every returned packet enters the raffle, even an empty one, and the classes that raise the most get a class party.
  • 🙌 Volunteers: timeslots are on ParentSquare. Please sign up if you are Raptor approved. Not able to volunteer? Come cheer.

More ways to raise money for the school, including the Fred Meyer and Office Depot programs, are on our fundraising page.

Thank you, Jamba Juice

We are excited to share that Jamba Juice has agreed to support the Walkerthon with post-run drinks for our students. After all those laps, a smoothie is going to hit the spot. Thank you, Jamba Juice, for backing our Wildcats.

Dates to know

  • Sept. 15 - The Pastathon at Pastini Cedar Hills, 20% back to William Walker
  • Sept. 21 - No school
  • Sept. 23 - Cafecito with Mr. B
  • Sept. 24 - Walkerthon
  • Oct. 7 - PTC meeting, 5:45 to 7:00 p.m.
  • Oct. 9 - No school
  • Oct. 13 - McMenamins Friends and Family Night, 50% back to William Walker
  • Oct. 21-22 - Conferences
  • Oct. 22-23 - No school
  • Oct. 29 - Trunk or Treat
  • Oct. 30 - No school

You can subscribe to the PTC calendar to get all of these on your phone. Questions? Email us at williamwalkerptc@gmail.com.
