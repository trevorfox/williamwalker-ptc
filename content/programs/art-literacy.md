Art Literacy is a long-standing art enrichment program in the Beaverton
School District, made possible by a crew of dedicated parent and community
volunteers and the financial support of our Parent Teacher Club.

Throughout the year, our volunteers introduce all students at William Walker
to six diverse artists and their unique techniques. Then students make their
own art production in that style.

Louise Nevelson's found object sculptures, Van Gogh's expressive
brushstrokes, the intricate beading of the Confederated Tribes of Grand
Ronde — over their years at William Walker, students build a foundation in
looking at and talking about art, and they experience a variety of materials
they may not otherwise encounter.

## What your support covers

- Supplies like paint, brushes, paper, sculpting materials, oil pastels, and drawing pencils for use across 25 classrooms
- Materials for sample projects to demonstrate techniques with students
- Copies of curriculum materials for volunteers to use in lesson presentations

No art or teaching experience is needed to volunteer — all training, lesson
plans, and materials are provided. And every dollar goes straight into
supplies to bring hands-on learning to every one of our students.
