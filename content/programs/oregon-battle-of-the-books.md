Oregon Battle of the Books (OBOB) is a voluntary, extracurricular reading
program for students in **grades 3 through 5**. Kids form teams of 4 or 5, read
or listen to books from this year's list of 16, and compete in quiz battles
about them. It builds reading and listening skills, teamwork, and good
sportsmanship. Even more importantly, it's fun.

## How the season works

- **November:** teams start meeting at periodic lunchtime book clubs. Every student memorizes all 16 titles and their authors by the end of the month.
- **Each team member** reads or listens to at least 4 of the 16 books and becomes the team's expert on them. Teams are encouraged to read as many as they can.
- **Week of January 11, 2027:** school battles begin. No notes are allowed at a battle.
- **First Saturday in March (usually):** the school champion team represents William Walker at the regional championship.

## How families help

Find the books at the school or public library, or buy them if your child
wants to study at home. If you can, get your child's team together after
school to practice. It makes a big difference. If your team wins the school
championship, you'll need to drive your student to the regional tournament.
