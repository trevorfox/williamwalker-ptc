## Thursday, October 29 · 6:00–7:30 PM

Trunk or Treat is a safe, festive fall evening for the whole William Walker
community. Families decorate their car trunks, fill them with treats, and kids
in costume go trunk to trunk, with no busy streets to cross and plenty of
friendly faces.

## Bring the family

Costumes are encouraged for kids and grown-ups alike. Younger and older
siblings are welcome. It's a great way to meet other families before the
holidays.

## Host a trunk

Want to decorate a trunk and hand out treats? Email
[williamwalkerptc@gmail.com](mailto:williamwalkerptc@gmail.com) and we'll save you
a spot. Any theme works, from spooky to silly.
