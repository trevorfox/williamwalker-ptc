Washington Square is giving away **up to $15,000 to local schools this year through
School Cents**, and William Walker is one of the schools in the running. How much of it
we get depends on one thing: how many of our families upload their receipts.

If you shop or eat at Washington Square at all between now and **March 31, 2027**, this
is the easiest fundraiser we have. There is nothing to buy that you were not already
buying. You just take a picture of the receipt.

## How it works

- **Step 1: Get the app.** Download School Cents for [iPhone](https://maceri.ch/SCiOS) or [Android](https://maceri.ch/SCAndroid), or use the website at [school-cents.com](https://www.school-cents.com). Create an account and choose **William Walker Elementary** as your school.
- **Step 2: Shop or dine at Washington Square.** Any store or restaurant in the center counts, and so do online orders you pick up in store and delivery orders placed through apps like DoorDash or UberEats.
- **Step 3: Upload the receipt.** Tap **Upload**, take a photo of the paper receipt or a screenshot of a digital one, and submit. That is the whole job.

**Every dollar you spend is 5 points for William Walker**, and you get a **2,500-point
bonus the first time you upload a receipt**. Points add up across the whole school, and
at the end of the program Washington Square splits the money among its schools based on
who earned the most.

## Double points in October

These Washington Square stores earn **2x points all month long**:

- Free People
- Hot Topic
- Pendleton
- Sephora
- Starbucks
- Yankee Candle

A Starbucks run on the way to the mall counts. Early holiday shopping at Sephora or
Pendleton counts double too.

## Triple points all season

These stores earn **3x points on every purchase from now through March 31**:

Abercrombie Kids, Apple, Bath & Body Works, Build-A-Bear Workshop, Champs, Dick's
Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, H&M, LEGO, lululemon, Macy's, Mango, Shiekh, Travis
Mathew, and Vuori.

If a new phone, winter sports gear, or a LEGO birthday present is in your future, buying
it at Washington Square and snapping the receipt turns it into real money for our
classrooms.

## A few details worth knowing

- **Most ways of paying count**, including debit, credit, gift cards, and SNAP/EBT. Loyalty rewards and trade-in credit do not earn points.
- **Credit card statements do not count.** It has to be the actual receipt or a screenshot of the digital one.
- **One school per account.** If you want to support a second school too, you need a second account under a different email.
- **We need to stay in the game.** Schools that have not turned in at least $500 in receipts by **February 1, 2027** can be dropped from the program. That is a low bar, and a few uploads from each family clears it easily.
