Next Tuesday, skip cooking. McMenamins Cedar Hills is hosting a **Friends & Family
Night benefiting William Walker Elementary** on **Tuesday, October 13, from 5 p.m. to
close**, and **50% of all dine-in sales go to the school.** That is half of every bill,
all evening, from every table in the pub.

![McMenamins Cedar Hills Friends & Family Night flyer: Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 5 p.m. to close, 50% of all dine-in sales go to William Walker Elementary School, 2885 SW Cedar Hills Blvd, Beaverton](/assets/blog/mcmenamins-night/flyer.png)

## The details

- 🗓️ **When:** Tuesday, October 13, 5 p.m. until close. McMenamins Cedar Hills is open until 11 p.m. on Tuesdays.
- 📍 **Where:** McMenamins Cedar Hills, 2885 SW Cedar Hills Blvd, Beaverton. [Get directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=McMenamins+Cedar+Hills+2885+SW+Cedar+Hills+Blvd+Beaverton+OR+97005).
- 🍽️ **Dine in.** The 50% applies to dine-in sales, so grab a table rather than ordering to go.
- 👋 **Nothing to bring.** You do not need a flyer or a code. The whole evening counts.

Kids are welcome, there is a [kids menu](https://www.mcmenamins.com/cedar-hills), and
the full menu is on the [McMenamins Cedar Hills page](https://www.mcmenamins.com/cedar-hills).
Bringing a bigger group? They take reservations by phone at (503) 641-0151.

## Half is a lot

Most restaurant fundraisers give back 10 to 20 percent. McMenamins is giving back half,
which means a family dinner that costs you $60 sends $30 to William Walker. That money
goes straight into this year's PTC programs, from Art Literacy to field trips to
teacher grants.

- **Bring the whole crew.** Grandparents, neighbors, coworkers, and your kid's whole soccer team all count. Every table in the pub does.
- **Make it a class night.** Text a few families from your classroom and claim a corner.
- **Go early if you can.** Tuesday at 5 p.m. is quieter than 6:30. If there is a wait, hang in there, since it all counts until close.

Thank you to McMenamins Cedar Hills for hosting us. Please tell your server thanks
while you are there.
