Pastini Cedar Hills Crossing is turning Tuesday into a fundraiser for William Walker.
Dine in or take out on **Tuesday, September 15**, mention William Walker Elementary
when you order, and **Pastini will donate 20% of your table's sales** to the PTC.
That is it. No flyer, no code, no sign-up.

## How it works

- 🗓️ **When:** Tuesday, September 15, all day. Pastini is open from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.
- 📍 **Where:** Pastini Cedar Hills Crossing, 3487 SW Cedar Hills Blvd, Beaverton. [Get directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fGeMeDD194kWdHMZ8).
- 🗣️ **What to do:** Tell your server or the person at the counter that you are with **William Walker**. That is what makes your table a participating table.
- 🥡 **Takeout counts too.** [Order online](https://order.pastini.com/order/pastini-cedar-hills-crossing-3487-sw-cedar-hills-blvd) or call (503) 619-2241, then mention William Walker when you pick up.

Planning ahead? [Browse the menu](https://www.pastini.com/pastini-menu/) or check
[hours and location details](https://www.pastini.com/locations/cedar-hills-crossing/).

## Make it a big night

Every participating table adds up, so the more of us who show up, the more the
school earns.

- **Bring the extended family.** Grandparents, neighbors, and coworkers all count. Anyone who mentions William Walker is in.
- **Go early or go takeout.** Pastini does not take reservations, so if the wait is long, order to go and eat at home.
- **Spread the word.** Forward this post or just text a few families from your kid's class.

Thank you to Pastini for hosting us. Please tell them so when you are there.

## Walkerthon is less than two weeks away

[Walkerthon](https://williamwalkerptc.com/programs/walkerthon), our biggest
fundraiser of the year, is **Thursday, September 24**. Fundraising is happening right
now, and it is the pledges that come in before the run that get us to our goal of
**$40,000**.

- 📩 **Packets are home.** Ask family, friends, and favorite local businesses. Have your kid make a short video. Check whether your employer matches donations.
- 💳 **Donate online through [Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/peer-to-peer/walkerthon--2026)**, or by cash or check. All donations are tax deductible, and Zeffy sends a receipt automatically. Our tax ID is 26-3542951.
- 🎟️ **Every returned packet enters the raffle**, even an empty one, and the classes that raise the most get a class party.
- 🙌 **Volunteers:** timeslots are on ParentSquare. Please sign up if you are Raptor approved. Not able to volunteer? Come cheer.

More ways to raise money for the school, including the Fred Meyer and Office Depot
programs, are on our [fundraising page](https://williamwalkerptc.com/fundraising).
