## Walkerthon is September 24

[Walkerthon](https://williamwalkerptc.com/programs/walkerthon) is our biggest
fundraiser of the year. It supports all of the PTC's programs, including Field Day,
Trunk or Treat, Art Literacy, Mystery Science, Reading Week, teacher grants, and
field trip access, along with the other services that make our school community
stronger. **Our goal this year is to raise $40,000.**

Student fundraising packets are coming home with students. Get creative with your
fundraising: have your kid make a video to send around, ask your favorite local
businesses, or check whether your employer offers a company match.

### How to donate

Donations can be made by cash, check, or online through [Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/peer-to-peer/walkerthon--2026).
All donations are tax deductible, and donations made through Zeffy get a receipt
automatically. Our tax ID is 26-3542951.

Every student who returns a packet is entered in a raffle, even if the packet is
empty, and the classes that raise the most money will get a class party. If you have
prizes to donate, email [williamwalkerptc@gmail.com](mailto:williamwalkerptc@gmail.com).

### Volunteers and spectators

## Other ways to support our students

These take a few minutes to set up and then work in the background all year.

- **[Fred Meyer Community Rewards](https://www.fredmeyer.com/communityrewards)** — Link your Fred Meyer Rewards Card to William Walker PTC and a slice of what you already spend comes back to the school, with no change to your fuel points or rewards. You need a Fred Meyer account, then enroll in Community Rewards by searching for William Walker PTC or using our NPO number **WB669**.
- **[Washington Square](https://www.shopwashingtonsquare.com/schoolcents)** — Shop or dine at Washington Square between now and March 31 and submit receipts at [school-cents.com](https://www.school-cents.com). William Walker will earn a piece of $15,000.
- **Office Depot** — Give William Walker's school ID **70243444** when you pay for any in-store or phone order, and the school earns credits worth 5% of your purchase.
- **[Box Tops for Education](https://boxtops4education.com/get-the-app)** — It is an app. Use referral code **DNCB7KA2** for an extra $10 bonus and pick William Walker Elementary as your school. Buy participating products and scan your receipt in the app within 14 days, or link your Walmart account and skip the scanning entirely.
- **Employer matching** — Many local companies match donations, including Adidas, Alaska Airlines, Apple, Columbia, Google, Intel, Kaiser, Microsoft, and Nike. This is extra valuable as we gear up for Walkerthon.

## Volunteer with us

Volunteering can be one hour or it can be more, during school hours or outside of
them. Some of the ways people help:

- Supervising field trips
- Presenting Art Literacy lessons six times a year
- Supporting teachers directly with prep work or small reading and math groups
- Joining the fundraising committee
- Helping at events like Walkerthon and Field Day

No experience is necessary for any volunteer role, though you do need to be approved
through Raptor. Whatever your talents, reach out to
[williamwalkervolunteer@gmail.com](mailto:williamwalkervolunteer@gmail.com) with questions.

## Join our next PTC meeting
