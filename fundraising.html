Fundraising

There are two ways to help fund a Wildcat: give directly, or let your everyday shopping earn for William Walker. Most of what’s on this page costs you nothing beyond a few minutes of setup.

Give directly

Walkerthon is our big one.

Walkerthon is the PTC’s signature fall fundraiser — the whole school runs, laps get counted, and the community rallies behind our Wildcats. It shapes the budget for the entire year, so what we raise in September decides what we can fund through June.

No extra cost

Earn for William Walker while you shop.

These programs send money to William Walker based on shopping you were going to do anyway. Each one takes a few minutes to set up once, then runs in the background.

Fred Meyer Community Rewards

Link your Fred Meyer Rewards Card to William Walker PTC and a slice of what you already spend comes back to the school, with no change to your fuel points or rewards. Sign in at fredmeyer.com, search for William Walker PTC or our NPO number WB669, then click Enroll. Your card has to be registered first, and you need to swipe it — or give the phone number attached to it — every time you shop for the purchase to count.

Enroll at Fred Meyer ↗

Washington Square

Help William Walker earn a share of $15,000! Shop or dine at Washington Square August 1 to March 31, 2027. It’s easy to support our school when you submit receipts at www.school-cents.com. View the latest School Cents program information, including special bonus point offers, school point standings, FAQ and more at the link below.

Office Depot — 5% back

Give William Walker’s school ID 70243444 when you pay, in any store or on a phone order, and William Walker earns credits worth 5% of your school-supply purchase. It works all year, not just at back-to-school. The ID has to be given at checkout — online orders can’t be credited.

Box Tops for Education

Box Tops is an app now — there’s nothing to clip. Buy participating products, scan your receipt in the app within 14 days, and pick William Walker Elementary as your school. If you shop at Walmart, you can link your accounts and skip the scanning entirely.

Where it goes

Every dollar stays at William Walker.

The PTC is a parent- and staff-run 501(c)(3). What we raise pays for art literacy, field trips, classroom funds, staff appreciation, and the events that bring our community together — for every student enrolled at the school, not just the ones whose families can give.
